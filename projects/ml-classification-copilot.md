# Agentic Classification Workflow for Pricing Adjustments

[← Back to Chad Marshall's portfolio](../README.md)

## Overview

I developed a Copilot-assisted, agentic classification workflow to analyze customer pricing adjustment cases and identify recurring root causes, responsible parties, and opportunities for process improvement. The monthly review began with more than 100 cases and expanded to hundreds of cases.

## The challenge

Reviewing pricing adjustment cases one by one makes it difficult to consistently identify patterns across a large set of cases. The goal was to create a repeatable way to summarize and categorize cases while keeping human review central to the process.

## Approach

1. **Prepare the data:** Organize the case information for review, using appropriately protected data.
2. **Generate initial summaries:** Use Copilot to create a first-pass, high-level summary of each case.
3. **Review and categorize:** Manually review the summaries and underlying cases, then group them by root cause and responsible party.
4. **Define classification criteria:** Develop detailed definitions for the categories so they can be applied more consistently to larger datasets.
5. **Expand the analysis:** Extend the workflow from the initial monthly review of 100+ cases to hundreds of cases, helping surface recurring patterns and potential process improvements.

## My contribution

I prepared the data, designed and refined the categorization approach, manually reviewed the initial summaries, and derived the detailed classification definitions. I also expanded the scope of the analysis to hundreds of cases. Copilot supported the summarization and classification workflow; human review informed the categories and definitions.
